## Pacing targets

| Milestone | Intensive (8 pokes/s) | Casual (4 pokes/s, half the time) | Mostly idle |
|---|---|---|---|
| First boss (Era II) | ~1 min | ~3–4 min | ~20 min |
| Era IV (prestige unlocks) | ~10 min | ~20 min | ~1.5 h |
| Era V | ~30 min | ~1–1.5 h | ~3.5 h |
| Era VII | ~2 h | ~5–7 h (faster with prestige) | — |

Design notes: clicking dominates the first minutes; from Era III on, minions and "% of production" pokes carry the
economy so idle play stays viable (roughly 3× slower than casual). Bosses are real gates: they need ~15–25% of the era
threshold in gasps within 30 s, so players occasionally have to build up and retry. Paranoia (prestige) is cube-root of
lifetime gasps, and the unspent bonus is softcapped past 250 points so late timelines plateau instead of exploding.
